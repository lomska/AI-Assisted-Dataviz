Do foreign guests sharpen the local tourist season?

Foreign guests’ timing makes the season clearly sharper in 105 regions and clearly flatter in 27: they smooth the year where locals crowd into August. In the typical region, however, the difference is small, about 0.015 on the Gini index; it reaches about 0.06 in regions where foreign guests are at least 70% of nights. This chapter compares the 254 regions with at least 10,000 nights from both foreign and domestic guests.
