Peaks and Plateaus: Europe’s Foreign Tourism Rhythms

Seasons of foreign guests in 259 regions, 2023–2024

How many months is enough to reach 75% of foreign visitors’ annual overnight stays? Croatia, Greece and Bulgaria need four; Czechia, Poland, Romania and Serbia need nine: foreign demand for their accommodation stays steady throughout the year.

Summer carries the year

Of 1.47 billion foreign nights a year in 2023–2024, 43% fell in June–August. In 33 of 34 countries the busiest month is July or August; only peaks in December.

Do foreign guests sharpen the local tourist season?

Foreign guests’ timing makes the season clearly sharper in 125 regions and clearly flatter in 49; in 83 it makes no clear difference. More foreign guests would change little: even 50% more foreign nights would move the typical region’s Gini index by about +0.003.
